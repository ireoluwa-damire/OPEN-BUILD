# Original mascot directions

Prepared for the OPEN BUILD learning and project-planning app, 27 October 2026. These are design proposals: animal traits below describe brand symbolism, not a scientific ranking of intelligence. Working names are provisional.

The companion should make a learner feel capable enough to try, safe enough to be wrong, and curious enough to return. It should support a seven-day calibration period and the longer journey from goal to completed project. Friendly motivation and expressive reactions are useful references; the identity, artwork, voice, and animations should be original.

## Recommendation: Sumi, the cuttlefish mentor

Sumi gives the product a useful visual metaphor for adaptation. A single small coral forehead spot becomes a simple status signal; the indigo body and teal frill give it a recognizable silhouette without requiring accessories. Its short arms can arrange lesson tiles, reveal a route, or hold a finished project. It can look thoughtful, pleased, puzzled, or reassuring without speaking.

Most importantly, Sumi fits an app that changes the plan as evidence arrives. The character can organize scattered tiles into a path after each assessment. This communicates what the app does more clearly than a mascot whose only role is applauding streaks. The app should explain that it is adapting pacing and practice from observed performance; neither mascot copy nor onboarding should label people as permanently fast or slow learners.

## Six directions

### 1. SUMI — adapt, explore, build

- **Species/metaphor:** Cuttlefish; flexible thinking and adaptation. Suitable for a personalized planner that becomes a tutor.
- **Silhouette:** Rounded bean-shaped body with a wavy side frill, three grouped arm shapes at the base, two cream eyes, one coral forehead dot. Readable without tiny details.
- **Palette:** Ink indigo `#34346B`, muted teal `#3EA9A0`, coral `#F47C65`; warm ivory eyes/background `#FFF5E7`.
- **Personality:** Calm, observant, curious; a companion experimenting alongside you. Says, “That clue helps. Let’s try a smaller step.”
- **Signature animations:** Frill ripples while thinking; arms rearrange task tiles when the plan changes; a little ink-free confetti puff marks a completed project. No alarmed reaction to wrong answers.
- **Best fit:** A distinctive identity centered on adaptive learning and project planning.
- **Tradeoff:** Some people may read the simplified character as a squid or octopus. Treat species precision as secondary to recognition, and avoid complicated tentacle rigs.
- **MVP pose:** Holds a single tile, looking toward the next action.

### 2. TAVI — notice, connect, solve

- **Species/metaphor:** Raven; a visual shorthand for cleverness, pattern recognition, and making connections.
- **Silhouette:** Compact upright ink-navy body, three-feather crest, small diamond-like amber beak, broad periwinkle wings. Two clean eye shapes carry expression.
- **Palette:** Ink navy `#24304B`, periwinkle `#8F9DEB`, amber `#E9AC4B`.
- **Personality:** Curious analyst with dry warmth. Says, “You spotted the pattern. Can you use it somewhere new?”
- **Signature animations:** Head tilt for a question; taps two tiles together for a connection; opens a wing to reveal a project milestone.
- **Best fit:** Coding, engineering, research, and other problem-solving disciplines; credible beside adult learning content.
- **Tradeoff:** Dark birds can look severe. Use rounded cheeks, visible warm eyes, soft wings, and a welcoming stance. Avoid a menacing brow.
- **MVP pose:** Holds a puzzle tile under one wing.

### 3. PIP — try, learn, leap

- **Species/metaphor:** Fox; curiosity, resourcefulness, and quick movement between small challenges.
- **Silhouette:** Large asymmetric ears, cream cheeks, compact terracotta body, broad curved tail forming an upward sweep.
- **Palette:** Terracotta `#D96B53`, cream `#FFE8CA`, navy `#28354A`.
- **Personality:** Energetic, playful, eager to test an idea. Says, “One small experiment. Then we’ll know more.”
- **Signature animations:** Ear flick when a hint arrives; short hop after a challenge; tail traces the next step on a path.
- **Best fit:** A lively, approachable learning app whose daily sessions feel quick and playful.
- **Tradeoff:** Fox mascots are common. Its tail silhouette and expressive ear asymmetry must do the distinctive work; avoid generic orange-fox logo styling.
- **MVP pose:** Looks back invitingly while holding a blue path marker.

### 4. MICA — recall, practice, grow

- **Species/metaphor:** Elephant; memory, patience, and steady accumulation of understanding.
- **Silhouette:** Rounded body, large petal ears, short expressive trunk, compact feet. A trunk curl acts like a visual question mark.
- **Palette:** Lavender grey `#9C94B9`, soft apricot `#F3BC98`, deep plum `#4C4168`.
- **Personality:** Patient, reassuring, quietly pleased by progress. Says, “It’s been a few days. Let’s see what stayed with you.”
- **Signature animations:** Trunk places a recalled idea into a stack; ears lift at a new connection; gently rolls a small building block into the next milestone.
- **Best fit:** Recall practice, confidence building, deliberate study, and welcoming learners who feel intimidated.
- **Tradeoff:** The memory metaphor is strong, but speed is less central. Keep the proportions lively and avoid presenting the character as a symbol of slowness.
- **MVP pose:** Balances one learning block on the trunk.

### 5. SPARK — notice the next idea

- **Species/metaphor:** Firefly; insight as a small light that becomes clearer through practice.
- **Silhouette:** Rounded midnight-blue head and body, two little wings, small warm glowing abdomen. The eyes and light remain legible at icon scale.
- **Palette:** Midnight `#25334E`, golden light `#FFD467`, dusty sky `#8CAEC8`.
- **Personality:** Gentle, inquisitive, optimistic. Says, “A little clearer than yesterday.”
- **Signature animations:** Soft glow when a concept clicks; traces a dotted route; settles beside the current lesson. Always provide a static equivalent and reduced-motion variant.
- **Best fit:** A broad discipline-neutral brand with a strong app-icon shape.
- **Tradeoff:** A generic lightbulb-like treatment would feel impersonal. Make the face and wing silhouette distinctive; avoid constant flashing or mapping brightness to a person’s intelligence.
- **MVP pose:** Hovers beside one illuminated next-step dot.

### 6. KITO — collect, connect, return

- **Species/metaphor:** Squirrel; gathering useful pieces, finding them again, and building something with them.
- **Silhouette:** Small rounded body, short tufted ears, oversized crescent tail. The tail can frame a lesson tile without changing species.
- **Palette:** Russet `#AD6B4B`, pale oat `#F2DBB5`, muted blue `#6A93B6`.
- **Personality:** Busy but organized; practical, resourceful, cheerful. Says, “Let’s keep the useful part and try it in your project.”
- **Signature animations:** Files a tile into a tail-framed stack; retrieves an old card for spaced review; lines up three project steps.
- **Best fit:** Resource recommendation, curated study collections, and project preparation.
- **Tradeoff:** “Collecting” can imply saving endless resources without using them. Pair every resource action with a tiny applied task.
- **MVP pose:** Offers a single useful tile, with no pile of distracting objects.

## Which direction fits which promise?

| Direction | Strongest product message | Natural motion language | Main design challenge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sumi | The plan adapts to your progress | Rearranging, unfolding, rippling | Simplify arm and frill details |
| Tavi | Understand patterns and solve problems | Tilting, revealing, connecting | Keep a dark bird welcoming |
| Pip | Make progress through quick experiments | Hopping, pointing, sweeping | Stand apart from other fox brands |
| Mica | Build lasting knowledge patiently | Stacking, recalling, lifting | Keep energy without pressure |
| Spark | Understanding becomes clearer | Glowing, tracing, hovering | Avoid a generic lightbulb and flashing |
| Kito | Gather and apply the right resources | Filing, retrieving, arranging | Reward use rather than collection |

## Sumi across the seven-day calibration

Give learners a useful provisional path from day one. Seven days should improve the estimate, not become a waiting room before learning starts.

| Moment | Mascot behavior | Example copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goal intake | Holds an empty route board | “What would you like to be able to do?” |
| First baseline | Shows one tile at a time | “Show me what you know. Guessing is useful too.” |
| New concept | Places a small tile on the route | “Try this idea, then use it.” |
| Wrong answer | Tilts thoughtfully; offers a smaller tile | “That tells us what to practice next.” |
| Delayed recall | Retrieves a familiar tile | “Let’s check what stayed with you.” |
| Plan adjustment | Reorders the next few tiles | “We’ll give this step more room.” |
| Day-seven summary | Opens a route assembled from observed work | “Here’s the route we can support so far.” |
| Project completion | Displays the learner’s finished artifact | “You made this. Ready for the next version?” |

Use a text explanation alongside every state; color and motion must never be the only signals. Let people mute the mascot and reminders. Progress copy should describe completed work and observed improvement, without shame for missed days or claims to measure innate intelligence.

## Smallest useful production kit

1. One front-facing neutral pose, one three-quarter pose, and a monochrome silhouette test at 24, 48, and 96 pixels.
2. Six expression variants: welcome, think, explain, encourage, celebrate, and gently refocus.
3. Four short animation loops: idle, thinking, hint, and milestone celebration. Include static and reduced-motion versions.
4. A 512-pixel app-icon study, notification avatar, empty state, and lesson-complete placement.
5. One palette specification with contrast-checked text colors; never place body copy directly on the character.
6. A compact voice guide: warm and specific, one sentence per interruption, celebrate effort plus demonstrated understanding, no guilt or exaggerated ability claims.

The accompanying board explores Sumi, Tavi, Pip, and Mica. It is a concept comparison, not a final animation model sheet. Choose one direction before commissioning consistent turnarounds, clean source artwork, and animation rigs.
